Sundays, 11:00

The English Service.

A full, separate service in English — same church, same building, same Bible-first foundation, in the language that's easiest for you to worship in.

What to expect

A full hour of worship, teaching and prayer, led in English from the same auditorium used for the Afrikaans services. Children's programmes run alongside, shared with the 09:00 service.

Who it's for

Anyone more comfortable worshipping in English — long-time members, newcomers to Pretoria, and visitors alike. No membership required to attend.

The English Service.

A warm, Word-based hour in English. Same church, same building, in Elardus Park, Pretoria. Come as you are.

When Sundays, 11:00 One hour, until 12:00
Where Main auditorium Elardus Park, Pretoria, 0181
Kids All ages A children's programme runs during the service

What to expect

A full hour of Word-based worship and teaching. Kids are welcome. The children's programme gives them a safe space to get to know the Lord while the service is on.

Free coffee is waiting afterwards, so there is time to talk. No booking, no dress code. If you need directions or a lift-friendly note for someone else, the office can help.

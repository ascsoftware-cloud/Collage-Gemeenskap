Sundays, 11:00am – 12:00pm

The English Service.

A warm and intimate, Word-based worship service in English — same church, same building, in Elardus Park, Pretoria.

What to expect

A full hour of Word-based worship and teaching. Kids are welcome — a dedicated children's programme runs alongside the service, giving kids a safe space to get to know the Lord.

After the service

Free coffee is available afterwards, to encourage connection and conversation between believers. No booking needed — just come as you are.
